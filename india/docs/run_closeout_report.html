India Mosaic Production Close-out

The annual Landsat mosaic run is effectively complete: 10,847 of 11,320 cell-years built (95.8%). The remaining 473 are honest archive gaps plus a handful of edge cases — not lost work. This is the story of the run, the "stuck export" incident that slowed it, and what resolved it.

The incident

Exports appeared to hang for hours

Partway through the run, a subset of mosaic exports sat in RUNNING for 6 to 47 hours — far past the ~41-minute median — and were cancelled automatically to free queue slots. At its worst this stalled roughly 28 of the 50 running slots, cutting throughput from ~50 to ~22 mosaics/hour.

The affected cells were scattered across the grid (NG-42…, NG-43…, NH-44…, NI-43…), not one region. Early hypothesis — a memory limit in the compositing graph — pointed the wrong way.

Root cause

Queue contention, not a bug

The cancelled tasks had been actively computing, not stuck. Their batchEecuUsageSeconds ranged from 35k to 450k at the moment of cancellation — the signature of healthy work competing for a shared worker pool.

The decisive test

A single dense cell (NG-43-X-D_2017, 250 granules) that had "hung" completed in 1.8 hours once the queue was empty — running at ~74 EECU/s versus ~13–21 EECU/s under contention. Same cell, same graph, five times faster.

Two earlier fixes were tested and ruled out: shardSize=32 (the limit is in the graph, not the tile) and cloud<30 (only cut 250 → 182 granules, still over the interactive-memory ceiling).

Resolution

Re-launch with an empty queue

With the queue drained, the 71 historically-stuck cell-years were re-queued. 69 of 71 completed — each in roughly two hours, one after another. The six-hour auto-cancel threshold was raised to 48 hours so slow-but-healthy exports were no longer cut off.

Two small follow-ups

NH-44-V-A_2021 and NI-43-Z-D_2019 stall partway through export (EECU freezes at 44% and 73%). Likely a specific scene; worth a targeted look, not urgent.

8 cells blocked by a physics bug

These cells have 1–6 granules but an empty overpass table, and the topographic-correction step crashes with NoneType has no len(). A small guard (treat an empty table as "no correction") would unblock all eight for free.

Both are minor against a 95.8% complete product; neither affects the mosaics already delivered.
